# ST73 — Comments delivered by who wrote them

**Priority:** 🟰 Medium · **State:** Accepted

**What a user gains.** Comments on a task's pull request reach the task according to who wrote them, so internal accounts and listed partners count and others do not.

**The test.** On a task's pull request, a comment of an internal account and of a listed partner reach the task at once; a comment of an unknown account is held and shown until the person releases it; a comment that claims another author changes nothing.

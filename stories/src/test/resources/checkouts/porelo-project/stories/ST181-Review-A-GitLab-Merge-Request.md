# ST181 — Review a GitLab merge request

**Priority:** 🔽 Low · **State:** Idea

**What a user gains.** A person can review a GitLab merge request, with its threads and suggestions in the waiting work and replies released back.

**The test.** Read GitLab threads and suggestions into the waiting work and release a reply back. Known authors follow the configured trust rule; an unknown author is held and a forged author cannot change that. A reply is sent only after its release.
